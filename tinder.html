Tinder Travel:
Passport 2.0

From a simple location-changer to a comprehensive travel companion finder. A feature evolution story unlocking new value without a new paywall.

Explore Live Prototype
tinder-travel.vercel.app

The Problem

People know where to travel. They don't know who to travel with. Existing options like Facebook groups, Reddit forums, or hostel common rooms are noisy, unscalable, and offer zero compatibility signals. Furthermore, meeting strangers in foreign cities is high-stakes; existing tools lack verification and safety infrastructure.

The Insight

"Passport already exists. It just doesn't do enough."

Today, Tinder Passport = change a map pin.
Passport 2.0 = find compatible people at your destination.

The Marketplace Model

Free Local Host (Supply)

Locals join for free and supply the network, giving them a clear reason to participate.

  • Create a local host profile.
  • Set services: food spots, hidden places, city tours, cultural experiences.
  • Appear in Gold/Platinum traveler feeds.
  • Build a trust score via verified connections.

Gold/Platinum Traveler (Demand)

Already paying subscribers unlock immense new utility via Travel Mode.

  • Set destination + travel dates.
  • Browse ranked compatible travelers.
  • View verified local host profiles.
  • Connect and chat safely in-app.

Why It Works

🚀 Distribution Moat

Available to hundreds of millions of Tinder users on day one. No separate download. No cold start from zero.

💰 No New Paywall

Gold/Platinum already includes Passport. Travel Mode is Passport 2.0 — more value, same subscription tier.

🔒 Safety by Default

Verified hosts, in-app chat, and public meeting guidance. Trust is the hardest thing to build, and Tinder already has the infrastructure.

⚖️ Fair Freemium

Locals join for free and supply the network. Travelers pay to access it. Both sides have a compelling, aligned reason to be here.
