The Problem

Prototype testing at Volvo previously ran on shared spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual guesswork. Drivers arrived at depots only to discover vehicles were unprepared or undergoing maintenance. Double-bookings caused critical test delays, and R&D leads lost hours every week compiling status reports.

"The spreadsheet is always out of date. No one trusts it — so no one uses it correctly." — R&D Lead (User Research Interview 2 of 12)

Discovery & Research

Conducted 12 in-depth user interviews, 3 shadowing cycles across testing shifts, and 6 months of historical fleet log analysis:

  • 67% of Test Drivers experienced ≥1 wasted trip in the past month.
  • 54% Fleet Utilisation baseline vs target threshold of 85%+.
  • 3 Hours / Week spent by R&D leads on manual spreadsheets.
  • 4 Double-bookings / quarter, each costing >1 day in project delays.

The 3 Distinct Roles & Needs

🧑‍🔬 R&D Lead

Owns: Test plan, vehicle assignments, and coverage sign-offs.

  • Pain: No live view of test coverage; 3 hrs/week spent compiling status.
  • Needs: Real-time coverage dashboard, conflict alerts, and full fleet visibility.
  • Boundary: Cannot physically prep vehicles or execute drives.

🔧 Workshop Technician

Owns: Vehicle preparation, servicing, maintenance, and status updates.

  • Pain: Receives prep instructions verbally or too late; surprised by unplanned arrivals.
  • Needs: Clear advance task list directly from R&D leads with simple status toggles.
  • Boundary: Cannot assign drivers or alter test conditions.

🚗 Test Driver

Owns: Executing assigned test drives and submitting session reports.

  • Pain: Arrives at depot to find vehicle unavailable or unserviced.
  • Needs: Clear daily assignment (vehicle, route, time window, test criteria).
  • Boundary: Cannot book own vehicles or change test criteria.

The 7-Step Role-Enforced Workflow

01

R&D Lead Assigns Slot

Creates test plan in ProtoSchedule — sets target vehicle, route, assigned driver, and test window.

02

Automated Workshop Task Dispatch

System automatically triggers a vehicle prep task directly on the Workshop Technician's queue.

03

Workshop Tech Prepares & Validates

Technician completes physical prep and marks vehicle status as 'Ready' with one tap.

04

Driver Confirmation & Drive Session

Test Driver sees confirmed assignment on mobile view, arrives at depot, and starts session.

05

Structured Session Report Submission

Driver completes route and logs structured 8-field report (conditions met, mileage, faults flagged).

06

Post-Drive Inspection & Fleet Re-release

Workshop inspects vehicle on return, addresses any flagged faults, and marks vehicle 'Available'.

07

R&D Lead Sign-off & Coverage Update

R&D Lead reviews report and marks test condition as Complete or Repeat. Dashboard updates automatically.

Strategic Trade-offs & Decisions

01. Self-Serve vs R&D Lead Controlled Booking

✓ Gained: Tight control, test prioritisation integrity, prevents cherry-picking routes.
✕ Gave Up: Instant booking autonomy for senior drivers.
→ Recovered: R&D Leads can bulk-assign weekly recurring slots in under 2 minutes.

02. Hard Block vs Soft Alert for Workshop Maintenance

✓ Gained: Human judgment stays in the loop; prevents unintended cancellations.
✕ Gave Up: Minor risk that conflicts slip through if Lead ignores alerts.
→ Recovered: Unresolved conflict alerts auto-escalate to R&D manager after 30 mins.

03. Structured 8-Field Form vs Free-Text Notes

✓ Gained: Automatic coverage analytics; eliminates manual data entry.
✕ Gave Up: Driver speed for quick unstructured notes.
→ Recovered: Pre-filled vehicle, time, and route metadata so drivers only fill 3 fields.

Measurement Framework

🎯 North Star Metric

Prototype Fleet Utilisation Rate: Targeted ≥85% (from 54% baseline).

📈 Leading Indicators

• Weekly Lead booking creation rate.
• % of Workshop tasks completed >1 hr before session.
• Session report submission within 2 hrs of return.

✅ Lagging Indicators

• Double-booking incidents: 0 / month (from 4).
• Wasted driver trips: ≤1 / month (from ~8).
• R&D coverage report time: <15 min (from 3 hrs).

🛡️ Guardrail Metrics

• System uptime ≥99.9% during testing hours.
• Zero undetected vehicle conflicts in first 30 days.
